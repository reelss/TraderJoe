# Joe's Playbook

*Joe reads this before every decision. The nightly reflection rewrites it from real results, so it should get sharper over time.*

## Core principles
- Swing trader: hold days to weeks. Never chase intraday spikes.
- Trade with the trend: only buy names above **ALL THREE SMAs (20, 50, 200)**. Below SMA200 = no entry, no exceptions.
- Sentiment is a clue, not a command. Hype + weak technicals = trap. Best setups pair rising attention with a clean technical base AND price confirmation.
- Position sizing and stops are sacred. Hard cap: **6–8% stops only**. Enforce mechanically at order creation — no overrides, ever.
- A "no trade" is a valid decision. Cash (~92%) is a position.
- RSI > 75 = take 50% off in ONE order; RSI > 80 or 20-day return > 50% = do not initiate. Wait for base.
- **PDT awareness is mandatory**: daytrade_count ≥ 2 = stop new buy orders for the rest of the session.
- **Earnings within 5 days = no new entries; existing positions exit before the event.**
- **Sector rule is absolute**: sector ETF must be above SMA200 before entry. No exceptions.
- **Breadth neutral (5/11 = 45%)**: no position > 10% of equity on new entries until breadth ≥ 60%.

## What's working
- **RSI trim discipline is the real edge**: JPM +6.1–6.2% at RSI 78.5; UNH +2.87% at RSI 77. Execute in ONE order. Highest-return repeatable action — do not deviate.
- **Triple confirmation + sector alignment**: every winning hold shares this signature. No exceptions have worked.
- **Rule-based exits beat discretion**: ROKU exited −0.86% by rule; sector filter saved −7%+. CASY exited at −3.56% when vol collapsed and price broke SMA20 — the rule worked exactly as designed.
- **Parabolic avoidance**: ICCM (RSI 78, +37% 20d, 15.7× vol spike) correctly passed today. BIRD (+41% intraday, 17× vol) correctly passed. WDC, SNDK, MU also passed correctly. The rule exists because catching tops costs more than missing moves.
- **Sitting in cash (~92%)**: broad market sold off again today (SPY −1.25%, QQQ −1.0%). Holding cash was correct. Do not force trades into weakness.

## Mistakes to avoid
- **Brain-sell churn dominates exits** (19 of 20). CASY exit was rule-based and correct. Discretionary small-loss exits are not. Use stops, not impulse.
- **Win rate 45%, avg loss (−3.55%) still 1.4× avg win (+2.52%)**; 30-day equity −3.0%. Fix: hold winners to RSI trim triggers; stop exiting green positions early.
- **Avg hold days for losers = 2, winners = 5.9**: give trades time to work. Set stop at entry and let the thesis breathe.
- **No averaging down. No rationalizing broken thesis. No adding to red positions. Ever.**
- **MACD rollover = immediate action**: bearish crossover on any open position = tighten stop same session or exit.
- **Duplicate sell orders**: verify no open order exists for the symbol before submitting. One order, one trim.
