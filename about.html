What is Joe?

Joe is a fully autonomous AI trading agent running on a $10,000 paper (simulated) account. He scans financial news and market data every hour during market hours, reasons over the candidates like a disciplined swing trader, and places real orders through Alpaca's paper trading API — all without any human intervention.

The brain is Claude (Anthropic's AI). Every decision Joe makes is logged verbatim — the reasoning, the conviction, the outcome. This is an experiment in whether a well-prompted LLM with good market data can trade profitably over time.

The Strategy

01
Swing trading. Joe holds positions for days to weeks — never chases intraday moves. He is not a day trader.
02
Trend filter. Only opens new longs when SPY is above its 200-day SMA. If the broad market is in a downtrend, Joe manages existing positions and waits.
03
VIX fear filter. When market fear (VIX) is elevated (>20), position sizes are cut. When fear is extreme (>25), no new entries at all.
04
Triple confirmation. A buy requires: price above 20 & 50-day SMA, bullish MACD, and RSI between 50–75. All three, or no trade.
05
Momentum ranking. Among valid setups, Joe prioritises names with the strongest 12-month relative momentum (Jegadeesh-Titman factor).
06
Volume confirmation. Entries are preferred when today's volume is 1.5× the 20-day average. A breakout on low volume is suspect.
07
Earnings avoidance. Joe never enters a new position within 5 days of earnings. Holding through a binary event is a coin flip, not a swing trade.
08
Sector diversification. No single sector can exceed 30% of invested equity. Correlated bets amplify losses.
09
ATR-based risk sizing. Each position is sized so entry-to-stop risk equals ~1% of equity. Volatile names get smaller positions and wider stops automatically.
10
PDT-aware. On a sub-$25k account, pattern day trading rules cap same-day round trips. Joe never sells a position opened the same day.

How Joe Learns

Every evening after market close, a stronger model (Sonnet) reviews the day's decisions and outcomes and rewrites Joe's playbook — a living document of lessons, what's working, and what to avoid. Joe reads the playbook before every decision the next day. Lessons compound over time.
